Fastest way to report a problem

Open Eve and shake your iPhone. A short report box appears. Type one line and send. Eve attaches the technical details so you do not have to describe them. It works offline and sends later.

Do I need an account?

You must be at least 18. There is no password. Eve starts with a private on-device journey and a background social identity. Private Eve works without Sign in with Apple. Talking with people (follows, Tap Ins, messages, circles, plans and posts) needs it, and it can restore that social identity on another supported iPhone. Private companion history uses a separate encrypted iCloud backup, and only if you turn that protection on.

Where is my data?

Your private conversations with Eve, memories, goals, check-ins, and mood history stay on your iPhone by default. Public profiles, relationships, posts, and encrypted message delivery use Eve's social service. Photos you post stay. You can keep up to 15 live photos on your public profile. Stories last 24 hours. Clips play for 90 days, then that clip post is gone. A chat or group chat holds 500 messages. Words you use to tune your feed, and alert choices such as quieting likes, stay on this iPhone. They are not sent with lookups or Feed Agent requests. See the Privacy Policy for the exact boundaries.

Can I turn lookups off?

Yes. The globe above the keyboard is on when you install Eve, and you can switch it off. With it off, Eve answers from what is already on your iPhone and says that it did not look anything up. A world question that does leave goes from your iPhone to public sources. It does not go through an Eve server.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Account → Delete account first asks Eve's service to delete the social account, then erases the local journey and media. If that cloud deletion cannot be confirmed, Eve leaves the local data in place so you can retry. If you protected your private journey, Eve also removes the encrypted iCloud backup and its recovery key. Deleting the app by itself can leave a cloud account or an encrypted backup behind.

A suspension of public posting is stored in this iPhone's Keychain. It is not part of the account. Deleting the account or the app does not clear it. Eve does not write that suspension to Apple's device-check service. A factory reset of the iPhone can clear it.

Who can see a plan?

A plan is private to the two of you by default. When you confirm one, you can choose Private, Friends, or Public. The other person's choice can only narrow that. Friends means people you have both tapped in with, on the companion's profile. Public means anyone who can already open that companion profile. A public plan is not a post on the public feed, and choosing it does not publish the app. Nothing is listed before the other person has answered. Agreeing on a plan does not require a purchase, and a companion does not invent a plan on its own.

Is anything for sale?

Eve 1.0 does not sell anything. The Amplify sheet may name Spark, Flux, Eve+, and Frontier. Checkout is locked, so none of those can be bought, and Frontier is not a live route. Amplify Studio is locked for now. Planning, your private companion, the feed, circles, and messages do not require a purchase. There is nothing to refund, because there is no checkout. If a later version sells something, the privacy policy and the terms will be updated before that sale.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Eve sends this iPhone's push token to Eve's service so it can deliver alerts. The token is not used for advertising. Alert choices you make in chat stay on this iPhone.

Why does Eve ask for Apple Health?

Only to read activity you already recorded, so your rings and context reflect real life. Eve never writes to Apple Health, and health data never leaves your iPhone. You can decline and use Eve normally.

What is the optional model download?

Eve works without it. If you choose to download an optional on-device model, it runs entirely on your iPhone and your conversations are never sent anywhere for it. Apple Music is the only music service Eve connects to.

What languages does Eve offer?

English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, and Japanese. A phone set to another language uses the first of those six on the phone's language list, or English.

I saw something that should not be on the feed

Tap the … menu on the post and choose Report post, or block the account from the same menu. Hiding a post removes it from your feed on this iPhone. A report follows the moderation path in the app. A public post is checked on the iPhone before it is shared. That check does not send the picture to a moderation company. You can also email the address above.

Reporting a safety or privacy concern

Email roobbyythompson@icloud.com and put Safety or Privacy in the subject line so it gets seen first.

If you are in crisis, contact a professional or call or text 988 in the United States. Eve can point to resources and cannot provide emergency help.
