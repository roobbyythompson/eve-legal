Fastest way to report a problem

Open Eve and shake your iPhone. A short report box appears — type one line and send. Eve attaches the boring technical details so you don’t have to describe them. It works offline and sends later.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Eve works immediately when you open it. There is no sign-up wall and no password. Sign in with Apple is optional and only used to protect and recover your journey.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. Your conversations with Eve, your memories, goals, check-ins, and mood history stay on the device. Only things you deliberately post to a feed or circle leave it. See the Privacy Policy for specifics.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Account → Delete & reset account. That erases your local profile, chats, memories, goals, circles, media, and any cloud account. It cannot be undone.

Why does Eve ask for Apple Health?

Only to read activity you already recorded, so your rings and context reflect real life. Eve never writes to Apple Health, and health data never leaves your iPhone. You can decline and use Eve normally.

What is the optional model download?

Eve works without it. If you choose to download an optional on-device model, it runs entirely on your iPhone and your conversations are never sent anywhere for it.

I saw something that shouldn’t be on the feed

Tap the … menu on the post and choose Report post, or block the account from the same menu. Reports are reviewed. You can also email the address above.
