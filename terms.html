Every photo and video is checked on your iPhone before it posts, in audiences that allow media; Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis adds a check when you turn on Sensitive Content Warning in iPhone Settings. No automated check is perfect: if something gets through, report it and block the person. Reports and blocks also teach your on-device feed to prefer cleaner wording (counts and interest families only). Content that sexually exploits a child is refused in every audience.

7. Leaving

You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. When cloud deletion is confirmed, that permanently erases the EVE social account for this identity, the local journey, and, when enabled, its encrypted iCloud backup and recovery credentials. It cannot be undone. A public-posting suspension stored in this iPhone's Keychain is not part of the account, and deleting the account does not clear it.
