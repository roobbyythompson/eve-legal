Eve — Privacy Policy

The short version: your private companion journey stays on your iPhone unless you explicitly protect it. Your EVE social account is separate and can be recovered with Sign in with Apple.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Eve doesn’t sell your data, doesn’t show ads, and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. Your private mood history, check-in notes, companion conversations, and durable memories stay on your device. Only content you explicitly choose to share enters a social audience.

1. Where your data lives

Your mood and energy logs, private companion conversations, Eve’s memories, goals, and private wellness notes are stored locally on your iPhone by default. Public profile fields, relationships, circles, posts, social media, and encrypted messaging delivery use Eve’s Supabase social service so the people and devices you selected can receive them. Optional private journey backup, Health, Calendar, weather, Feed Agents, and other connections are described below.

2. AI choices

Eve’s companion relationship layer, safety routing, memory, and device-action approvals operate on your iPhone without requiring a separate model download. On supported devices, Eve may use Apple’s on-device intelligence for language generation. You can also choose an optional compatible model from Eve’s private model library; once downloaded, that model runs on your iPhone.

Frontier intelligence and live web is not offered for purchase to ordinary users in Eve 1.0. If an authorized account explicitly enables that separate route, Eve sends the current, explicit web-search question and nothing else through Eve’s authenticated Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI. Eve does not include recent turns, identity, name, handle, profile, purpose, strengths, goals, memories, Health data, Calendar events, faith or cultural notes, social feed, or location with the request. A pseudonymous safety identifier may accompany it, and Eve requests that the model API not store the response.

If you choose to download an optional model, your iPhone downloads those files directly from the third-party host that publishes them. That host can see your device’s network request for the files, the same as any download. Your conversations, memories, and personal context are never sent there. Chat scrollback and Eve’s growth memories remain stored locally, and you can clear the conversation or delete memories at any time.

Eve also uses Apple’s on-device NaturalLanguage framework to help recognize language that may indicate you’re in distress, so it can point you to real support resources. This analysis runs entirely on your device before any model route and is not stored separately from the message itself.

3. Health data

If you connect Apple Health, Eve reads activity data (steps, energy, exercise, sleep, water, mindful minutes, workouts) only with your permission, to show your fitness rings and personal context. Eve never writes to Apple Health — it does not create, edit, or delete entries in your health record. Health data never leaves your device, is never shared with anyone, is never used for advertising, and is never sold — period, and this applies even if you later disconnect Health or delete Eve.

4. Calendar

If you connect Calendar, Eve reads plain event titles and start times for the next 48 hours only, to give the companion sensible timing for check-ins and to mention what’s coming up. Eve never reads event notes, locations, or attendees. When you explicitly ask Eve to schedule something, it creates a local draft containing only the event title and proposed time and opens Apple’s Calendar editor. Nothing is added until you review the details and tap Add; Eve does not silently edit or delete existing events. Calendar data stays on your device and is used only inside your own chat context.

5. Location and weather

If you enable automatic atmosphere and allow location access, Eve requests an approximate location (about kilometer-level accuracy). Latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to return current weather, and Apple location services may provide a nearby city label. Eve does not use this information for advertising or tracking. You can turn automatic atmosphere off and deny location access at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Spotify

Spotify account connection is locked and marked Coming soon in Eve 1.0. This version does not start Spotify authorization, request Spotify account data, publish a Spotify soundtrack on profiles, or receive Spotify OAuth tokens. Eve’s separate on-device vibe catalog contains metadata only and does not contain or play audio.

7. EVE accounts, Feed Agents, and Supabase

Eve uses Supabase Auth and its social service for account identifiers, public names and handles, profile fields you choose to publish, relationships and circles, posts, encrypted message delivery, moderation, and rate limits. Before Sign in with Apple, Eve may use a passwordless anonymous Supabase Auth identifier.

When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a stable account identifier and may provide an Apple private-relay email address. Supabase stores that authentication information linked to your EVE account. Eve does not display the relay email on your profile or use it for advertising.

Feed Agents send only the catalog keys for agents you selected to retrieve public publisher content. Your custom agent names, engagement learning, private companion chat, memories, notes, Health, Calendar, media, and location are not sent with Feed Agent requests. Sharing an item uses Apple’s share sheet with the publisher’s canonical link and no EVE personalization data attached.

8. Friend and group messages

Eve supports live direct and group messaging. Message text, captions, photos, and supported short videos are encrypted on the sending iPhone before upload. The delivery service and private media bucket store ciphertext rather than readable content.

The service stores public device-key packages, conversation membership, sender and recipient identifiers, content type, timestamps, expiration, and delivery/read receipts to route messages. Those routing records are metadata and are not end-to-end encrypted. A newly added or recovered device can send and receive new messages after registering its key, but it may not decrypt messages or media encrypted before that device key existed. Eve cannot prevent a recipient from saving, recording, or screenshotting content.

9. Optional journey recovery

Recovery has two separate layers. First, Sign in with Apple authenticates your permanent EVE account through Supabase. On another supported iPhone signed into the same Apple account, that can restore your public profile, handle, social graph, feed access, and the ability to use a fresh companion and send new messages even when iCloud Drive or iCloud Keychain is turned off. Native Sign in with Apple uses the Apple account available on that device; it is not a password field for an arbitrary Apple account on an unsigned-in device.

Second, if you choose Protect private journey with iCloud, Eve encrypts your core journey on your iPhone with AES-GCM before saving ciphertext in your private iCloud Drive, while a random key is synchronized through iCloud Keychain. This includes profile, check-ins, companion chat, missions, memories, progress, and learned support preferences. Photos, videos, social DMs, and historical message device keys are not included.

If iCloud Drive or Keychain is unavailable during Apple account recovery, Eve keeps the social account usable but marks the old private journey as waiting for iCloud. It does not generate a replacement key or upload a fresh backup over the protected journey.

10. What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data. We don’t show ads. We don’t use App Tracking Transparency because we don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we don’t share your data with data brokers or advertising networks. Your private mood history, check-in notes, and durable memories stay on your device. Private companion chat stays on-device unless you explicitly enable Frontier, in which case only the bounded request described above leaves the device. Only content you explicitly choose to share enters a social audience.

11. What others in the app can see

Your public profile link can show your name, handle, join date, and public profile fields. New accounts are private by default. Public-account posts can be viewed by other EVE users; private-account posts require the relationship level shown in the app. Check-ins stay private by default. If you explicitly share vibe and charge with circles, joined circles receive only that coarse signal. You separately control charge, friends, replies, AI-companion links, and media visibility.

12. Purchases

Eve 1.0 does not offer a checkout, in-app purchase, or subscription for sale. Amplify and Frontier are locked and marked as coming soon. If a later version offers Apple In-App Purchases, this policy will be updated before sale; Eve will not receive your card number or billing address from Apple.

13. Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete & reset account first requests deletion of the authenticated Supabase account and its EVE social data, then permanently erases the local journey and media. If cloud deletion cannot be confirmed, Eve leaves local credentials and data intact so you can retry. If private journey recovery was enabled, Eve also removes the encrypted iCloud backup and recovery credentials. Deleting the app without this option may leave a cloud account or encrypted iCloud backup in place.

14. Children

Eve is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it. Parents who believe their child has used Eve can contact us at the address below.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because almost everything Eve stores lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly, at any time, through Settings — viewing your data in the Brain tab, editing it in Edit Profile, or erasing it with Delete & reset account. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

16. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects your data, the app will tell you. The latest version lives in Settings → Legal and at this public URL.
